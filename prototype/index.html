Climate Change Explained

  • Climate change means long-term shifts in temperature, rainfall, and weather patterns across the planet.
  • One major cause is the increase of greenhouse gases, which trap heat in the atmosphere.
  • Human activities such as burning coal, oil, and gas add more of these gases to the air.
  • The effects can include hotter summers, stronger storms, rising sea levels, and changes to food production.
  • People can respond by saving energy, using cleaner transport, reducing waste, and supporting climate-aware decisions.
